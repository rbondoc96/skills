# Vocabulary, kanji, and grammar notes

Read before writing the 漢字・単語 and 文法 sections.

## Choosing words

Pull from the summary itself. Favor words that are common in Japanese,
useful in technical work, likely to recur, and worth learning as a whole
unit. Skip the obscure.

## Entry format

### <ruby>更新<rt>こうしん</rt></ruby> — update / refresh

**Meaning here:** To update or refresh something.

**Kanji:**

- **更** — change, renew
- **新** — new

**How the word works:** 更新 is making something current or new again.

**In software:** Updating data, records, packages, state, cached values.

Keep each entry tight. In `hard` mode, write these explanations in Japanese
with a short English gloss.

## Kanji rules

Character breakdowns exist to aid recognition and memory, so explain what
each character contributes *to this word*.

- Do not present those contributions as historical etymology unless it is
  known with confidence. Useful associations, not invented mnemonics.
- Do not imply a character has one fixed meaning or reading.
- Teach the word's reading (「更新 is read こうしん」) rather than isolated
  on'yomi and kun'yomi, unless the reading itself is the point.

Whole vocabulary items come before isolated character readings.

## Grammar

At most one pattern per lesson, chosen because it appeared naturally in the
summary. Omit the section entirely when nothing is worth the space.

Cover the pattern, its meaning, how it functions in that sentence, and one
short extra example from a technical context.

### ～ようになる

Something "comes to be" — becomes possible, or starts happening.

From the summary:

> トークンを自動的に更新できる**ようになりました**。

The behavior was not possible before the change and is now.

> エラーの内容を詳しく確認できるようになりました。
> It is now possible to inspect the error details.
