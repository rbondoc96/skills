# Investigation

Use this template when uncertainty is the issue's main subject. The output should support a later decision, not prescribe an implementation prematurely. Replace bracketed prompts with known facts and remove unused sections.

Title: `Investigate [specific uncertainty]`

```md
[Explain what is unknown, why resolving it matters, and which decision or follow-up work the answer will unblock.]

- [The relevant evidence or system behavior is documented.]

- [Recommended next steps and their tradeoffs are captured in a linked Linear document when substantial.]

## Constraints

- [State limits on time, access, environments, tools, or acceptable risk.]

## Affected Areas

- [Name the systems, workflows, or stakeholders the investigation must cover.]

## Out of Scope

- [Name implementation or adjacent research excluded from this investigation.]

## Questions

- [State one concrete question the investigation must answer.]

- [Link to existing evidence, reports, issues, documents, code, or external references.]
```
